II

Four Ways to Read One Passage

The Church reads Scripture in four senses. The literal sense is the ground floor. The other three are built on it (CCC 115-117).

I

LiteralWhat really happened

Jesus took the Twelve to Caesarea Philippi, pagan country, home to a shrine of the god Pan and a temple Herod built for Caesar. There He asked what people were saying about Him. Then He made it personal: “But who do you say that I am?”

Peter answered for all of them. Jesus gave Simon a new name, Peter, the Rock. Five verses later the same Peter tried to talk Jesus out of the cross and heard, “Get behind me, Satan!”

Peter = petros (original Greek). Peter is the rock upon which the Church was built. He was renamed from Simon to Peter. Anytime God renames someone, it means that person has a completely new and divine identity, mission, and "upgrade". 

Original Greek word for Church is Ekklesia means gathering of people. Once you're part of the Church, you are assured eternal protection against even all of Hell as long as you remain in it "..and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it".

“You are thinking not as God does, but as human beings do.”

MATTHEW 16:23
II

AllegoricalHow it points to Christ and His Church

Christ is the living Stone, and the Church is built on Peter and the faith he confessed. The gates of the netherworld will not beat it (CCC 552).

The keys stand for authority to govern God’s house: to forgive sins, teach and guide (CCC 553). That office of Peter continues today in the Pope (CCC 881).

“I will give you the keys to the kingdom of heaven.”

MATTHEW 16:19
III

MoralHow it tells us to act

Peter knew the right answer and still ran. On the night of the Passion he denied Him three times, swearing, “I do not know the man!” Knowing the right answer about Jesus does not mean you will live it.

Even the demons know who Jesus is. Faith is more than a correct idea in your head. “Faith is a personal adherence of the whole man to God who reveals himself” (CCC 176). It takes your mind, your will, your habits, your weekends, your phone, your friendships. All of you.

“Even the demons believe that and tremble.”

JAMES 2:19

“I do not know the man!”

MATTHEW 26:72
IV

AnagogicalWhere it leads

What we confess about Jesus here on earth, He confesses about us before the Father. The opposite is also true.

“Everyone who acknowledges me before others I will acknowledge before my heavenly Father.”

MATTHEW 10:32

“Whoever is ashamed of me and of my words, the Son of Man will be ashamed of when he comes in his glory and in the glory of the Father and of the holy angels.”

LUKE 9:26

The Christian ideal has not been tried and found wanting. It has been found difficult; and left untried.

Cases You Should Know

Pakistan · 2024

In Sargodha a crowd of nearly 2,000 attacked Nazir Masih, 74, over a claim he desecrated Quran pages. They burned his home and beat him with stones and sticks. He died about a week later. Everyone arrested was freed within weeks.

Pakistan · 2023

In Jaranwala a mob burned churches and Christian homes after a rumor about torn Quran pages.

China · 2025-26

Police detained nearly 30 pastors and staff of Zion Church in Beijing, the biggest crackdown since 2018. Eight leaders were still held as of July 2026.

Vietnam · 2026

Pastor Y Nuen Ayun, from the Montagnard hill tribes, was sentenced to 7 years 6 months. Pastor Y Quynh Bdap got 10 years.

Indonesia · 2025

In Padang a group stormed a Christian prayer house during children’s Sunday school. Two children, 8 and 11, were hurt.

France · 2016

Fr. Jacques Hamel, 85, had his throat cut while saying Mass near Rouen. His two killers had pledged loyalty to the Islamic State.

France · 2020

Three people were killed with a knife inside the Notre-Dame Basilica in Nice. The killer said he wanted revenge for Muslims killed by Western countries.

IV

They Are Not Ashamed. Why Are We?

Many Orthodox Jewish teens wear a kippah to school and keep the Sabbath. Many Muslim students step away to pray during the school day and fast through Ramadan, in public, without apologizing.

Boko Haram, ISIS and al-Shabaab go further and kill for their cause. We reject that violence completely, because a martyr dies for Christ and never kills for Him. Will we not even live for Him? Will we not even make the Sign of the Cross at lunch?

VI

Questions to Keep Asking Yourself

Come back to these when you pray this week.

Who is Jesus to you, honestly? Not the Sunday school answer.
Where is the gap between what you were taught about Jesus and what you actually believe?
Peter got the right answer and then denied Him. Does getting the right answer mean you will live it?
Beyond knowing about Jesus, what is stopping you from knowing Him?
What makes it hardest for you to practice your faith in public?
Christians, even children, are dying rather than deny Christ. Why would we deny Him where we are free?

Read It Again

Matthew 16:13-28

13When Jesus went into the region of Caesarea Philippi he asked his disciples, “Who do people say that the Son of Man is?” 14They replied, “Some say John the Baptist, others Elijah, still others Jeremiah or one of the prophets.” 15He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?” 16Simon Peter said in reply, “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God.” 17Jesus said to him in reply, “Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah. For flesh and blood has not revealed this to you, but my heavenly Father. 18And so I say to you, you are Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of the netherworld shall not prevail against it. 19I will give you the keys to the kingdom of heaven. Whatever you bind on earth shall be bound in heaven; and whatever you loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.” 20Then he strictly ordered his disciples to tell no one that he was the Messiah.

21From that time on, Jesus began to show his disciples that he must go to Jerusalem and suffer greatly from the elders, the chief priests, and the scribes, and be killed and on the third day be raised. 22Then Peter took him aside and began to rebuke him, “God forbid, Lord! No such thing shall ever happen to you.” 23He turned and said to Peter, “Get behind me, Satan! You are an obstacle to me. You are thinking not as God does, but as human beings do.”

24Then Jesus said to his disciples, “Whoever wishes to come after me must deny himself, take up his cross, and follow me. 25For whoever wishes to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for my sake will find it. 26What profit would there be for one to gain the whole world and forfeit his life? Or what can one give in exchange for his life? 27For the Son of Man will come with his angels in his Father’s glory, and then he will repay everyone according to his conduct. 28Amen, I say to you, there are some standing here who will not taste death until they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom.”
